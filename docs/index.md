# Where to begin

The PlayStation 4 version of *Bloodborne*, running natively on Linux and the Steam Deck: the
game's own code runs on the CPU and its graphics are translated to Vulkan. You need your own dump
of the game with update 1.09; no game files are included.

## What you get

- **Online and co-op**: messages, bloodstains, bells and summons, and **seamless co-op** that
  lasts through deaths, lamps and areas, with Linux and Windows players together.
- **Local co-op**: 2 to 4 players on one PC, each with their own controller.
- **Hunter's Atlas**: a fullscreen in-game map with fast travel to the lamps you have found.
- **Frame rate unlocked** or capped at 30, 60 or 90, from **720p to 4K**, and ultrawide screens.
- **FSR 3.1, FSR 4 and DLSS** upscaling.
- **Any controller**, with its own button prompts in the game, and **keyboard and mouse**.
- **Trophies**, **Discord** status and **Add to Steam** in one click.
- An **AppImage** for any distribution and the Steam Deck, which **updates itself**.
